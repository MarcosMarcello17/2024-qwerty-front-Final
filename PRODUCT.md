# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Individuals tracking personal finances. They open the app to log daily spending, review where their money goes, split costs with friends or roommates through groups, and set budgets by category. The user values speed and clarity over bells and whistles; they want to record a transaction in seconds and move on.

## Product Purpose

A personal finance tracker that makes spending visible and actionable. Users log transactions, assign them to categories and payment methods, set monthly budgets, share expenses through groups, and earn achievements for building good habits. Success looks like a user who actually keeps using the app because it's faster and less annoying than a spreadsheet.

## Brand Personality

Practical, simple, effective. The interface stays out of the way. No unnecessary decoration, no motivational fluff. Every element earns its space by helping the user understand or act on their finances. Confidence comes from clarity, not from visual noise.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI-generated UI: identical card grids, gradient text, glassmorphism, hero-metric templates with big numbers and gradient accents. If it looks like a prompt asked for "modern finance dashboard," it has failed.
- Over-designed fintech: apps that prioritize looking impressive over being usable.
- Gamified-first interfaces where achievements and rewards overshadow the core utility.
- Cookie-cutter SaaS dashboards with teal/purple gradients and rounded-everything.

## Design Principles

1. **Utility first**: every pixel serves a function. Decoration that doesn't aid comprehension gets cut.
2. **Speed of use over speed of understanding**: optimize for the returning user who logs transactions daily, not for first-time impressions.
3. **Honest data**: present numbers without spin. No vanity metrics, no misleading scales, no hiding bad months.
4. **Quiet confidence**: the app should feel like a well-made tool, not a performance. Quality shows in spacing, alignment, and type, not in effects.
5. **Friction-aware**: the fewer taps to log a transaction, the better. Reduce ceremony everywhere.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No specific WCAG target requested. Follow sensible defaults: sufficient color contrast, keyboard navigability, legible type sizes, and respect for prefers-reduced-motion.
